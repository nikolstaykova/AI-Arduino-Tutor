Building real circuits

The app checks circuits with a physics model, but real electronics can still go wrong. Use low voltages (like an Arduino's 5 V), follow the safety notes, and ask an adult for help with soldering irons and heat guns. You build at your own risk.

Lessons you create

Lessons you create are shared with everyone who uses the app, with your name as the maker. Only create lessons you're happy to share, and don't include anything offensive or anyone's personal information. When you create from a tutorial link, the lesson credits and links the original.
